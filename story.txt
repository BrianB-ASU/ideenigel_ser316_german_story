One morning, a sleepy Hund walked into our classroom and stole the teacher's chair.
Meanwhile, the entire classroom was distracted by an uncontrollable Ohrwurm playing over the radio.
Rammstein was playing a sick guitar solo that had everyone headbanging.
The teacher offered the dog a Broetchen, hoping it would finally give back her chair.
The classroom Klimaanlage suddenly gave out a loud bang, and the Hund promptly dropped
 the Broetchen to chew on the Handschuhe instead. The teacher sighed, grabbed a Staubsauger
 to clean up the mess, and muttered that this was the most wettbewerbsfaehig school in town.
